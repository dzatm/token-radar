What would a launch be worth?

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks, ranked by what its token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. A protocol is listed as recording no token — never as having none: the record is not proof. The board covers the house watchlist of 40 and the strongest pre-token protocols below it.

Model radar-2

implied = TVL × 0.25 × (1 + clamp(7d change, −80%, +150%) × 0.6) × (0.65 + min(age, 1095d) ÷ 1095 × 0.35) × youth × chains × sector

Youth is 0.85 under 90 days, else 1. Chains add 2% per extra chain, at most four. Sector: RWA 1.15, Risk Curators / Capital Allocators 1.10, Yield / Basis 1.05, Derivatives 0.95, else 1.00. No protocol name is special-cased. Change the sliders and the board re-ranks live.
